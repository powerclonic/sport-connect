# Project PRD: SportConnect

## 1. Overview
SportConnect is a mobile application designed to bridge the gap between sports enthusiasts. It serves as a community hub where users can discover, organize, and join local sporting events. The app focuses on accessibility, trust (through ratings), and community engagement.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Athletes:** Individuals looking for partners or teams for casual or competitive sports.
- **Organizers:** People who take the initiative to set up matches and manage participants.
- **Future Segment (Businesses):** Sports facilities and venues (courts, gyms, complexes) looking to manage bookings and attract players.

## 3. Core Features
- **Dynamic Onboarding:** A high-impact entry screen that separates the athlete experience from future business offerings.
- **Activity Feed:** A geo-located feed of upcoming matches (Soccer, Tennis, Running, etc.) with real-time status (spots left, distance).
- **Event Management:** Tools for creating public or private events, setting recurrence, and specifying location details with map integration.
- **Reputation System:** Dual-sided ratings for both players and organizers to ensure reliability and fair play.
- **Real-time Communication:** Dedicated chat rooms for every event to coordinate logistics.
- **User Personalization:** Comprehensive profiles showcasing favorite sports, skill levels, and detailed settings for notifications, privacy, and theme (Dark/Light).

## 4. Visual Identity & UX Principles
- **Style:** Clean, minimalist, and energetic.
- **Primary Color:** SportConnect Orange (#FF6B00) - used for action-oriented elements and brand recognition.
- **Typography:** Lexend (Sans-serif) - bold for impact, clear for readability.
- **UX Pillars:**
    - **Speed to Play:** Minimize clicks between opening the app and joining a game.
    - **Trust:** Transparent feedback and verified reliability scores.
    - **Clarity:** Distinct visual hierarchy to handle complex event data.

## 5. Information Architecture (Screen Map)
1. **Entry Page:** Brand introduction and login/register gateway.
2. **Activity Feed (Home):** Discovery engine with sports-based filtering.
3. **Event Details:** Deep dive into specific match info, participant lists, and maps.
4. **Create Event:** Multi-step form for match organization.
5. **Athlete Profile:** Personal stats, favorite sports, and community feedback.
6. **Chat:** Communication hub for match coordination.
7. **Settings Hub:** Centralized management for account, privacy, and appearance.
    - *Sub-screens:* Notifications, Privacy & Security, Theme Selection.

## 6. Future Roadmap
- **For Businesses:** Integration for court owners to manage schedules and payments.
- **Advanced Search:** Filtering by specific skill levels or complex availability.
- **Gamification:** Badges, streaks, and local leaderboards.
